# Project-owned native conversation consent form

## Study purpose

This study collects native Hindi and Telugu conversational audio and transcripts to evaluate emotional conversation systems. The study is not a medical or psychological treatment service.

## What participation involves

I understand that I may take part in a recorded multi-turn conversation in Hindi, Telugu, or a natural mixed-language style. I may skip any topic, stop at any time, or request withdrawal according to the retention policy explained by the researcher.

## Data collected

The project may collect conversation audio, native-language transcripts, language/script metadata, anonymized annotation labels, and model-evaluation judgments. The project will not intentionally request my name, contact details, precise location, account numbers, or unnecessary identifying information.

## Uses

I select each permitted use separately:

- [ ] Quality review and transcription
- [ ] Research evaluation
- [ ] Model training
- [ ] Voice-model evaluation
- [ ] Anonymous aggregate reporting

The researcher must not enable a use that I have not selected. Third-party datasets and project-owned data must remain separately governed.

## Privacy and retention

Raw audio and unredacted transcripts are restricted to authorized project personnel. Public reports use anonymized IDs and aggregate results. The researcher will explain the retention period, deletion process, and withdrawal contact before recording.

## Voluntary participation

I understand that participation is voluntary, that I may decline to answer, and that declining or stopping has no penalty.
