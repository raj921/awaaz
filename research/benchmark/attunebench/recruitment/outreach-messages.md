## Where to send (suggested channels)

- Personal WhatsApp network (highest trust, best conversion)
- College groups: language departments (Telugu dept, Hindi sabha), CS/AI student groups
- Community orgs: Telugu associations (TANA/ATA local chapters style), cultural groups
- Apartment/colony WhatsApp groups for demographic spread
- Avoid: public posts that promise payment without consent context — keep it warm and personal

## Batch plan

- Wave 0 (pilot): 6-10 conversations across 2-3 languages -> send transcripts to the pipeline (validate.py) -> fix friction
- Wave 1: grow to 100 conversations, keep language/topic/speaker balance in the tracker
- Wave 2: complete to 200, stop recruiting, focus on annotation quality
